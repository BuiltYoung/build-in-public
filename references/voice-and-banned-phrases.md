# Voice and banned phrases

Read this before writing any draft.

## Capturing the founder's voice

Build in public only works if the posts sound like the person. Readers can tell when a founder's account starts sounding like a marketing team, and they stop trusting it.

- **Use the voice samples in the profile.** Notice sentence length, formality, emoji use, humour, words they repeat, and how they talk about customers. Match those, not a generic "engaging" style.
- **No samples yet:** write plainly, in short sentences, like explaining something to a smart friend. After the first draft, ask "Does this sound like you? What would you change?" and record what they change as voice notes in the profile. Their edits teach you more than any questionnaire.
- **Don't raise the temperature.** Never make founders sound more excited, certain or successful than they are. Doubt is allowed and often engaging: "Not sure this will work, but here's why I'm trying it."
- **Match the platform's register without losing the person.** The same founder writes differently on LinkedIn and in a WeChat group, but it should still be recognisably them.
- **Keep their words.** When the raw material contains a sharp phrase of theirs, use it rather than polishing it away.

## Why these phrases are banned

They do three kinds of damage. They signal "advertisement" or "an AI wrote this", which makes people scroll. They replace a specific, checkable claim with a vague one. And they make every company sound the same, which defeats the point of telling your own story. The fix is almost always the same: replace the phrase with the concrete fact it was hiding.

Exceptions: quoting a customer who actually used the word, or when the word is literally true and specific (a garment that is literally seamless).

## Categories (apply these to any language)

1. **Hype superlatives:** claiming to be the best or first without proof
2. **Vague transformation verbs:** promising change without saying what changes
3. **Fake urgency or scarcity:** pressure that isn't real
4. **Engagement bait:** asking for reactions instead of earning them
5. **Filler framing and AI tells:** stock openers and sentence shapes
6. **Inflated scale:** big words standing in for numbers

For languages not listed below, apply the six categories to that language's common marketing clichés.

## English

- **Hype:** revolutionary, game-changing, game-changer, disruptive, cutting-edge, next-level, world-class, best-in-class, industry-leading, innovative (about yourself), the future of [X], this changes everything, nobody is talking about this
- **Vague transformation:** empower, unlock (your potential), unleash, elevate, supercharge, transform, revolutionize, leverage (as a verb), streamline, seamless, robust, holistic, synergy, one-stop shop, all-in-one solution, paradigm shift
- **Fake urgency:** don't miss out, act now, last chance, only a few spots left, limited time (unless true, and then say exactly how many and until when)
- **Engagement bait:** "Agree?", "Thoughts?" as the only question, "Read that again.", "Comment YES if…", "Like if you…", "Tag someone who…", "Unpopular opinion:" for a popular opinion
- **Filler and AI tells:** In today's fast-paced world, In the ever-evolving landscape, Let's dive in, Buckle up, Here's the thing:, It's not just X, it's Y, Excited to announce, Thrilled to share, Humbled to, on this journey (as filler), delve, testament to, tapestry, navigate (the landscape), game plan, secret sauce, magic
- **Inflated scale:** massive, huge, insane growth, skyrocketing, exploding, crushing it, 10x (unless literally ten times)

## 中文

- **夸大吹嘘：** 颠覆、革命性、重磅、炸裂、王炸、天花板、遥遥领先、史上最、全网首发、行业领先、划时代、yyds、绝绝子
- **空洞赋能：** 赋能、助力、打造、抓手、闭环、打通、链路、底层逻辑、顶层设计、颗粒度、对齐、沉淀、生态、矩阵、降维打击、弯道超车、全方位、一站式解决方案、开启新篇章、引领
- **虚假紧迫：** 速看、手慢无、错过再等一年、不看后悔、最后机会、限时限量（除非真实，并写明具体数量和截止时间）
- **互动诱饵：** 点赞收藏不迷路、评论区扣1、建议收藏（滥用时）、懂的都懂、你们觉得呢？（作为唯一的问题时）
- **套话和 AI 腔：** 在这个快速发展的时代、众所周知、不得不说、让我们一起、未来已来、干货满满、话不多说直接上干货、不是……而是……（反复使用时）
- **夸大规模：** 爆款（自称）、爆单、疯狂增长、火爆全网、刷屏

**小红书 note:** some casual address words (姐妹们, 宝子们, 家人们) are native to the platform. Use them only if the founder talks that way in their own samples; otherwise they read as borrowed.

## Rewrites

| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| "Our revolutionary app transforms how students study." | "Our app turns your class notes into 10 quiz questions. 212 students tried it last week." |
| "Excited to announce our seamless new checkout!" | "Checkout used to take 4 screens. Now it's 1. Here's what we cut." |
| "Insane growth this month 🚀" | "Waitlist went from 80 to 310 this month. Almost all of it came from one post in a gardening forum." |
| "重磅！全新升级，赋能每一位用户！" | "这周改了3个地方，最大的一个：记账从5步变成2步。" |
| "干货满满，建议收藏！" | "开店第一个月，我们算错了3笔账，这里是每一笔。" |
