# Build in Public: user manual

This skill helps you share the real story of building your business (what you tried, what you decided, what broke, what customers said) and turns it into posts, plans and community conversations. You don't need any social media experience. It works in any language: write to it in yours.

## Your first session

It takes about five minutes.

1. **Say what you want, in your own words.** You don't need to say "build in public". For example:
   - "I run a small pottery studio and want to start posting on Instagram. Where do I start?"
   - "I'm building an app for dog walkers. What should I post this week?"
   - "我开了一家面包店，想在小红书上记录创业过程。"
2. **Mention something that happened recently.** "Our first wholesale order came in" or "the new flavour sold out by noon". If you include it, the skill writes your first draft straight away.
3. **Answer up to five quick questions:**
   - What you sell, and to whom
   - Your stage: idea, building, beta, just launched, or growing
   - Where you want to post ("not sure" is fine; it will recommend two platforms)
   - How much you want to share (see "Choosing how much to share")
   - Optional: something you've written, like a message to a friend, so posts sound like you
4. **Check the defaults it used.** It tells you in one line, for example "3 posts a week, process only, first person". Change any of them by saying so.
5. **Save your profile.** At the end you get a block of text starting with `BUILD IN PUBLIC PROFILE`. Copy it into a notes app or document. This is what lets you pick up where you left off.

## Every session after that

Paste your profile first, then ask. The skill confirms what it picked up in one line and goes straight to work; it won't ask you the same questions again.

When something changes (a new stage, a new platform, a story you've posted), it gives you an updated profile. Save the new one over the old one.

## What you can ask for

| You want | Try saying | You get |
|---|---|---|
| Posts from your week | "Here's what happened this week: [notes, numbers, customer messages]. Turn it into posts." | A list of the stories in your notes, and drafts for the best ones |
| A post for one platform | "Write a LinkedIn post about why we dropped the free plan." | A ready-to-paste draft (see "Reading a draft") |
| A content calendar | "Plan my next 4 weeks. I have about 2 hours a week." | A table of dates, platforms, topics, and what material to collect |
| Strategy for your situation | "I'm pre-launch with no product yet. What do I even post?" | An approach for your stage and industry |
| Community help | "How should I reply to this comment?" / "Help me set up a WhatsApp group for regulars." | Reply drafts, comment prompts, polls, and group setup |
| A launch plan | "We launch on the 3rd of next month. Plan the run-up and launch day." | Week-by-week warm-up, a launch-day order, and the two weeks after |
| A performance review | "Here are last month's numbers for my posts. What should I change?" | What worked and didn't, with your numbers, three changes, and a new plan |

Platforms covered: X, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, 小红书 (RED), Threads, Reddit, plus Discord, WeChat and WhatsApp groups. You choose which ones.

## Choosing how much to share

You pick a level once, and every draft respects it. If you don't choose, it starts at Level 1, because you can always share more later but can't take something back.

| Level | Shares | Example detail |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Process only | What you did, learned and decided | "12 customer calls", "3rd redesign", "day 41" |
| 2 · Some metrics | Level 1 plus users, orders, growth, conversion | "310 on the waitlist", "orders up 40%" |
| 3 · Full openness | Level 2 plus revenue, costs, margins | "$1,200 a month in revenue, and where it comes from" |

If your notes include a number above your level, the skill leaves it out and asks whether you want to share it this once.

## Reading a draft

Each draft comes in the same shape:

- **Header:** platform, type of post, and a suggested posting window
- **Hook:** the opening line, shown separately so you can swap it
- **Post:** ready to copy and paste
- **Visual:** a specific photo, screenshot or clip to make
- **Hashtags:** sized to the platform, or "none" where they don't help
- **Before posting:** reminders, only when they apply (get someone's permission, check a sensitive detail)

Text in **[square brackets]** is a gap only you can fill, like `[NUMBER: how many pre-orders?]`. Replace it before posting, or tell the skill the answer and it will finish the draft.

Posting windows are starting points. After three or four weeks, check your own analytics and adjust.

## Your profile

The profile is a short record of your business, audience, platforms, sharing level, things you never share, your voice, and the stories you've already used (so posts don't repeat). It stays under about 300 words so it's quick to paste.

- Keep the latest version somewhere easy to find.
- Edit it yourself if something is wrong; the skill uses whatever you paste.
- Lost it? Say so, and the skill will ask the key questions again.

## Getting better drafts

The skill writes from your material, so better material means better posts.

- **Give specifics.** "Sold 23 jars at the Saturday market, mostly the smoked one" beats "good market day".
- **Use exact words.** Paste what a customer actually said, not a summary.
- **Include what went wrong.** Honest setbacks are often your most-read posts.
- **Say what you'd change.** "Too formal", "I'd never say 'excited'". It learns your voice from your edits.
- **Paste raw material as it is.** Messy notes, chat logs and screenshots of numbers are all fine.

If your material is thin, the skill asks two or three pointed questions instead of writing something generic. Answer them, or say "draft it anyway" to get a version with placeholders.

## What the skill won't do

- **Invent anything.** No made-up numbers, quotes, customers or results. Your audience's trust is the whole point, and one invented detail can cost it.
- **Share beyond your level** without asking you first.
- **Post for you.** It writes drafts; you decide what goes out.
- **Promise a "best time to post".** It suggests windows to test, because there is no universal answer.
- **Skip the reminders.** It asks you to get permission before naming or showing a customer or staff member, and flags details a competitor would love to see (unreleased plans, live pricing tests, supplier names).
- **Use marketing clichés** like "game-changing", "excited to announce", "赋能" or "重磅". It replaces them with the specific fact they were hiding.

## Troubleshooting

| What happened | What to do |
|---|---|
| It asked questions instead of writing | Your notes were missing a concrete detail. Answer the questions, or say "draft it anyway". |
| My revenue number isn't in the post | It's above your sharing level. Say "share it this once" or "change my level to 3". |
| The posts don't sound like me | Paste something you've written, and say what to change. Save the updated profile it gives you. |
| It keeps suggesting platforms I don't use | Tell it your platforms; they're saved in your profile. |
| A hashtag or length limit seems wrong | Platforms change their rules. These were checked in October 2026; follow what the app shows you. |
| It started from scratch | Paste your profile at the start of the session. |
