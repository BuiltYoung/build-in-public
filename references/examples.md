## 1. X · B2B SaaS · Decision

*Fernwick: no-show prediction for physio clinics. Building stage, Level 1.*

**Weak**
> Big week at Fernwick! 🚀 We've been working hard on some game-changing features for clinics. Stay tuned! #startup #SaaS #buildinpublic

**Strong**
> Killed the feature I spent 3 weeks on.
>
> Clinics told me they wanted online booking. Then 11 of the 14 I called said their real loss is no-shows, not bookings.
>
> Booking is out. No-show reminders are in. Week 7 starts over.

**Why it works:** the first line is a surprising, specific fact. It shows a decision and what it cost. Process numbers (3 weeks, 11 of 14) make it concrete without sharing any business metrics.

## 2. LinkedIn · Service business · Failure retro

*Northfold Advisory: operations consulting for small manufacturers. Growth stage, Level 3.*

**Weak**
> Failure is just a stepping stone to success. This quarter taught me so much about resilience and growth. Grateful for the journey. #leadership #lessons

**Strong**
> I underpriced a project by $8,000 last quarter.
>
> The brief was "map the order process for a 40-person metal shop." I quoted 3 weeks. It took 6, because nobody had written down how rush orders really worked. Two people knew, and one was on leave.
>
> What I'm changing:
> → A paid 2-day discovery before any fixed quote
> → Asking "who's the one person who knows how this really works?" on the first call
>
> The client was happy. My margin wasn't.
>
> If you price project work: how do you handle scope that only shows up halfway through?

**Why it works:** a specific loss in the first line (it fits before "see more"). The cause is concrete and doesn't blame the client. The changes are actionable, and the closing question invites peers to share expertise rather than just react.

## 3. Instagram · DTC ceramics · Behind the scenes

*Marlow & Kiln: handmade mugs sold online. Just launched, Level 2.*

**Weak**
> Handcrafted with love ✨ Our beautiful new collection is here! Shop now, link in bio. #ceramics #handmade #pottery #shopsmall #love

**Strong**

Visual: a 4-slide carousel. (1) 12 mugs on the shelf, the 4 cracked ones circled. (2) Close-up of a crack at the handle. (3) The glaze notebook page. (4) The 8 survivors, packed.

> 12 mugs went into the kiln on Sunday. 8 came out.
>
> Every crack started at the handle. My guess: I attached the handles when the clay was too dry, so they shrank at different speeds.
>
> Next batch, I'm attaching handles an hour earlier and keeping them under plastic overnight. I'll post the count.
>
> The 8 that made it are part of the 23 orders going out this week.

Hashtags: #handmadeceramics #potterystudio #kilnopening

**Why it works:** an honest failure rate, a lesson most buyers didn't know, a promise to report back, and a Level 2 number (23 orders) used naturally rather than as a boast. Three specific hashtags, within Instagram's limit of 5.

## 4. TikTok · Consumer app · Milestone

*Streakling: habit tracker. Beta, Level 2.*

**Weak**
> POV: you're building the next big app 🔥 Our habit app is changing lives! Download now 🙌

**Strong (script)**
- **Hook (spoken and on screen):** "300 people tested my habit app. 40% quit on the same day."
- **Beat 1** (screen recording of the retention chart, day 3 highlighted): "Day 3. Every time."
- **Beat 2** (talking head): "I asked 12 of them why. Most said they missed one day, lost their streak, and starting over felt pointless."
- **Beat 3** (screen recording of the new feature): "So now you get one rest day a week that doesn't break your streak."
- **Close:** "Shipping it Friday. I'll tell you if day 3 gets better."
- On-screen captions throughout. Hashtags: #habittracker #appdev #buildinpublic

**Why it works:** the hook is a surprising number, the middle shows evidence and reasoning, and the close gives viewers a reason to follow for the result.

## 5. 小红书 · Local bakery (pre-opening) · Invitation

*半糖面包铺: a neighbourhood bakery, 21 days before opening. Level 1. Written in Chinese because the audience is.*

**Weak**
> 标题：重磅！我们的面包店即将开业🔥
>
> 家人们！我们的面包店马上要开啦！用料天花板，口感绝绝子，开业有惊喜，不要错过哦～ #面包 #美食 #开业

**Strong**

封面大字：开业前第21天｜碱水面包试了7次

> 标题：开面包店前，碱水面包我试了7次
>
> 距离开业还有21天，这周只做了一件事：碱水面包。
>
> 第1–3次：颜色太浅，像普通餐包😅
> 第4–5次：颜色对了，但盐粒撒早了，烤完全化了
> 第6次：终于像样，家里人说太咸
> 第7次：盐减了三分之一，送给隔壁花店试吃，她说"明天还想要"
>
> 现在卡在一个问题：开业那周想上两款咸面包，碱水定了，另一款还在纠结👇
> A. 芝士火腿可颂
> B. 葱香肉松卷
>
> 评论区告诉我你会买哪个，票多的开业当天先上架，我会回来更新结果。
>
> #创业日记 #开面包店 #烘焙日记 #面包

**Why it works:** the cover and title carry a specific number, which is how people decide to tap on 小红书. The body is a real process log (Level 1 process numbers), the ask is a concrete choice with a promised follow-up, and it uses none of the clichés the weak version leans on (重磅, 天花板, 绝绝子, 家人们).

## 6. Threads · Creator · Full-openness metrics

*The Slow Ledger: a personal-finance newsletter for freelancers. Level 3.*

**Weak**
> Newsletter update: things are going great! Revenue is up and the community is growing. So grateful 🙏

**Strong**
> Month 9 of The Slow Ledger:
> 1,840 subscribers. 112 paid at $6/month = $672.
>
> Most new paid subscribers came from one post: "what I set aside from every invoice."
>
> Writing two more like it this month to see if that holds.

**Why it works:** Level 3 numbers with the arithmetic shown, the likely cause named, and the next experiment stated. It fits comfortably under 500 characters.

## 7. Reddit · B2B idea stage · Asking for input

*Quarrybell: an idea for tracking material deliveries for small contractors. Idea stage, Level 1. Posted in a community for contractors whose rules allow research questions.*

**Weak**
> Title: Check out my new app that will revolutionize construction management!
>
> [link] Features: real-time tracking, AI insights, seamless integration…

**Strong**
> Title: Contractors, how do you catch deliveries that arrive short? Researching before I build anything
>
> Disclosure: I'm thinking about building a tool for this, but there's nothing to sell and no link.
>
> Last month I talked to 9 small contractors. 7 had a story about a delivery that came up short and nobody noticed until the crew needed the material. Most track deliveries with paper slips or photos on their phone.
>
> If you run crews:
> 1. When a delivery is short, how do you usually find out?
> 2. What does it cost you when you don't?
>
> I'll post a summary of the answers here next week, whatever they say.

**Why it works:** it follows the community's norms (disclosure, no link, real question), uses process numbers from actual conversations, and promises to give something back.

## 8. WeChat group · Consumer app beta · Community message

*栗米记账: a budgeting app in beta, with a WeChat group of testers. Level 1.*

**Weak**
> 各位内测用户好！我们的产品经过全新升级，体验更丝滑，功能更强大，欢迎大家体验并多多支持！🎉🎉🎉

**Strong (sent as two short messages)**
> 大家好，我是栗米记账的小周。上周群里有6位朋友说"记一笔要点太多下"，我们数了一下，确实要5步。

> 今天的新版本改成了2步：打开 → 输入金额，分类会自动猜。想请大家帮个忙：今天记3笔账，然后回我一个1到5的分数，5分是"很顺手"。周五我把结果发到群里。

**Why it works:** it credits the group's own feedback, states the change in numbers (5 steps to 2), makes a small, specific ask, and commits to closing the loop. Splitting it in two matches how people read group chats.

## 9. Instagram Reel · Local yoga studio · Customer story

*Saltmeadow Yoga: a neighbourhood studio. Growth stage, Level 1.*

**Weak**
> Our amazing students love us! 💕 Join the best yoga studio in town. Transform your life today! #yoga #wellness #fitness #selfcare #love

**Strong**

Visual: a 20-second Reel of Mei rolling up her mat after class.

> Mei came to her first class in March and left after 15 minutes. Her back hurt, and she decided yoga wasn't for her.
>
> She came back because we added a chair-based class at 6pm, after three people asked for one.
>
> Last week she did the full 60-minute class. Her words: "I didn't think about my back once."
>
> Chair class: Tuesdays and Thursdays, 6pm. Mei says hi.

Hashtags: #chairyoga #beginneryoga #[neighbourhood]yoga

Before posting: get Mei's OK for her name, the video, and the quote.

**Why it works:** a real arc in a real person's words, the studio's decision shown as the turning point, and no health promise (it reports her experience; it doesn't claim yoga fixes backs). The consent note is part of the draft, not an afterthought.

## 10. One story at three transparency levels

*Pinewhistle Candles: an online candle shop that just got its first wholesale order.*

**Level 1 · Process only**
> A gift shop asked to stock our candles. First time anyone has bought them to resell instead of to burn. It took 4 emails, 2 sample boxes, and one awkward call about minimum orders.

**Level 2 · Some metrics**
> A gift shop just ordered 60 of our candles. That's more than our entire first month of online orders (41). It took 4 emails, 2 sample boxes, and one awkward call about minimum orders.

**Level 3 · Full openness**
> A gift shop ordered 60 candles at $14 wholesale: $840. At retail that's $1,500, so I gave up $660 of margin for a shelf in a shop about 200 people walk past every day. Here's why I think it's worth it.

**Why it works:** each version meets the concrete-detail rule. Level 1 uses process numbers, Level 2 adds counts, Level 3 adds money with the trade-off explained. The story doesn't get weaker at lower levels; it just gets different specifics.
